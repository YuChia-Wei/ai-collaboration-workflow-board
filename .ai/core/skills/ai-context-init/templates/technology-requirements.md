# Technology requirements

Record owner-selected technology constraints and their reasons. For each material
constraint, identify its authority, scope and observable acceptance when available.
Distinguish a dependency observed in source from an approved requirement.

Describe applicable deployment, compatibility, operating and resource constraints.
Keep unknown versions, environments and external services unresolved. Do not copy
another project's stack, provider settings or machine-specific limitations.

Link current implementation evidence separately and retain decisions still needed.
